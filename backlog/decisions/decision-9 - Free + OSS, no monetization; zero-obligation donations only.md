## Context

Commercial-viability study (COMMERCIAL_VIABILITY_REPORT §0/§4/§6): owner has RAL >EUR 45k, barring regime forfettario (EUR 35k ceiling confirmed 2026); ordinary P.IVA means ~24% INPS + 33-43% marginal IRPEF + ~EUR 1.2k/yr accounting, net-negative below ~EUR 3k/yr gross; and a free more-active fork (Epilogue) undercuts any paid tier.

## Decision

Stay free + GPLv3. Add GitHub Sponsors/Ko-fi with no tiers, no promised perks, treated as occasional income. Keep IAP plumbing dormant (reaffirms [[decision-4]]). Revisit ONLY if ALL of: >=~5k MAU; R4 differentiators shipped and ahead of Epilogue; owner willing to run a P.IVA; employer/CCNL cleared — then a EUR 5.99-7.99 one-time unlock on a new Play listing, F-Droid stays free.

## Consequences

Zero fiscal/compliance overhead now; the effort that would go to monetization goes to agentic-first capability instead ([[decision-10]]); revisit triggers preserved.
